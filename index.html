1. General Maintenance Instructions

Interval / Operating Hours Maintenance Instructions Status Remarks
Daily
(8–12 h)
Before starting work / beginning of shift		 Perform a visual inspection.
Check all protection devices and guards individually to ensure proper function.
Check all EMERGENCY STOP buttons individually to ensure proper function.
Clean all light barriers.
Listen for changes in gearing and motor sounds.
Remove accumulated paper and/or cardboard dust.
Clean or replace filters in pressure/vacuum pumps.
Weekly
(40–60 h)		 Clean the machine thoroughly.
Check and, if necessary, retension all chain and belt drives.
Check all gears and motors with separate oil filling for leaks.
Check the central lubricating system for leaks.
Check setscrews for firm seating and retighten if necessary, following the machine alignment instructions.
Monthly
(160–200 h)		 Check that overload protection devices function safely and reliably.
Check the oil level on the drive motor.
Lubricate all adjustment chains and spindles. Lubricant: Syn Temp 2000.
